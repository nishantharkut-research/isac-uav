# Reference Materials

The handoff archive keeps three kinds of source material separate:

- The journal PDF is the mathematical and experimental authority for the
  reproduction target.
- `references/problem.png` is an additional optimization-problem proposal. It
  is not silently merged into the journal objective or used to generate the
  paper figures.
- `references/Results_Comparison.docx` is historical diagnostic material from
  an earlier run. Its plots are not accepted results and its values are not
  used by parameter selection, training, plotting, or validation.

Accepted outputs must be generated from the final source, carry the selected
profile digest, and pass the preparation and full-run validation gates described
in `REPRODUCTION_GUIDE.md`.
